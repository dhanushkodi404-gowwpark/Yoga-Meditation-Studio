QUESTIONS & INTENTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know before stepping into the studio sanctuary.

I am a complete beginner — which session is best for my first visit?
We recommend either our Deep Restorative Yin or the 432 Hz Sound Bath. Both classes require zero prior yoga experience and are fully guided with bolsters, blankets, and gentle supportive alignment.
Do I need to bring my own yoga mat and props?
No, all studio props are provided complimentary with every booking. We supply premium non-toxic Manduka Pro cork mats, organic cotton bolsters, French linen straps, and weighted eye pillows. You are also welcome to bring your personal mat.
What should I wear to sound healing sessions?
Wear soft, comfortable, breathable clothing (such as loungewear, leggings, or sweatpants) and warm socks. Because your body temperature naturally drops during deep restorative states, we also provide plush weighted fleece blankets.
How small are the classes? Will it feel crowded?
Every studio class is strictly capped at a maximum of 14 practitioners. This ensures ample personal spatial freedom, clear sightlines, and personalized hands-on guidance from our master guides.
