OUR BEGINNING & INTENTION

Born from the Search for Stillness.

In 2018, founders Elena and James left their high-pressure Manhattan careers after experiencing severe chronic burnout. Their journey took them through the ashrams of Rishikesh, the sound temples of Nepal, and the restorative bathhouses of Kyoto.

"We realized modern wellness had become another performance metric. We wanted a shala where you don't come to achieve or burn out, but simply to unravel and listen."

Together, they designed a sanctuary that honors classical 8-limb Ashtanga principles while embracing modern neuroscience, circadian daylight rhythms, and somatic bodywork.
