Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about preparing for your first class or private sound journey.

Do I need to bring my own yoga mat and towel?
No. We provide complimentary medical-grade Portuguese cork yoga mats, organic cotton straps, blocks, and lavender-infused eucalyptus towels. If you prefer to bring your personal mat, our staff is happy to store it in your assigned locker.
Can beginners attend the 432 Hz Sound Baths?
Absolutely. Our sound healing baths require zero yoga experience or physical exertion. You simply rest comfortably on a cushioned mat with blankets and eye pillows while acoustic frequencies wash over your body.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
You can cancel or reschedule any class reservation up to 12 hours prior to the start time with full credit returned to your account. Private 1-on-1 sessions require a 24-hour notice.
Are private showers and luxury amenities available?
Yes. We have private limestone rain shower suites stocked with organic botanical shampoo, cedar body wash, Dyson Supersonic hair dryers, and plush Turkish cotton bath sheets.
Can I book the shala for private wellness retreats or corporate sessions?
Yes. During off-peak hours and weekends, the entire sanctuary is available for exclusive private buyouts, executive nervous system retreats, mindful wellness mornings, and acoustic recordings.
